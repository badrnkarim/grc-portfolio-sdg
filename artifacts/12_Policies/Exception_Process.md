# Policy Exception Process — SDG

Owner/Approver: Badr Karim  
Version: v1.0  
Effective date: 2026-02-28  
Purpose: Provide a formal, auditable method to approve temporary exceptions to policies/controls.

## 1) When an exception is allowed
An exception may be requested when:
- a control cannot be met temporarily due to technical constraints, or
- a short-term business need requires deviation, and
- compensating controls are possible.

Exceptions must be temporary and expire.

## 2) Mandatory exception fields
Every exception request must include:
- policy/control impacted
- justification and scope (what is affected)
- risk summary (impact + likelihood)
- compensating controls
- start date and expiry date
- owner and approver

## 3) Approval rules (US-grade)
- High/Critical risk exceptions require explicit approval and documented compensating controls.
- Exceptions without expiry are not allowed.
- Exceptions must be reviewed monthly; expired exceptions must be closed or renewed with re-approval.

## 4) Exception workflow
1) Requestor completes Exception Request Form.
2) GRC Owner assesses risk and recommends compensating controls.
3) Owner/Approver approves or rejects.
4) Exception is recorded in Policy Inventory and linked to evidence.
5) Monthly review and closure/renewal decision.

## 5) Evidence requirements
- Completed Exception Request Form
- Evidence of compensating controls (if approved)
- Record of monthly review and closure decision
