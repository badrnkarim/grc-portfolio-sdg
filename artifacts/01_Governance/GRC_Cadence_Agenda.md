# GRC Cadence & Agenda — SDG

Owner: Badr Karim  
Purpose: Define repeatable governance rhythm and audit-friendly meeting outputs.

## Weekly GRC Ops (30–45 minutes)
Inputs:
- Evidence Tracker (freshness, overdue items)
- Risk Register (new/changed risks)
- Findings Register + MAP Tracker (open items, due dates)
- Log Review Checklist (weekly completion)

Agenda:
1) Evidence freshness snapshot (what is overdue and why)
2) New risks / changes to risk ratings (LxI)
3) Findings and remediation status (High/Medium first)
4) Weekly log review completion + notable anomalies
5) Upcoming changes/demos requiring change-review evidence
6) Decisions: risk acceptance / exception approvals (if any)

Outputs (stored as evidence):
- dated notes or ticket updates
- updated due dates/owners in GRC_Master.xlsx

## Monthly Assurance Review (45–60 minutes)
Inputs:
- KPI/KRI Pack (metrics and trend)
- Control Matrix / SoA (coverage + changes)
- SOC 2 control tests status (CT-01/02/03)
- Vendor Register (TPRM) + open remediation
- Obligations Register (ISO 37301)

Agenda:
1) KPI/KRI dashboard summary (what improved, what worsened)
2) Control health & evidence coverage
3) Open High risks and High findings (aging + blockers)
4) Vendor risk status + next review schedule
5) Compliance obligations changes and actions
6) Next-month priorities and resourcing plan

Outputs (stored as evidence):
- Monthly GRC Report (PDF)
- Updated Evidence Tracker / MAP / Risk Register
