# COBIT 2019 — Governance Scorecard (SDG)

Owner: Badr Karim  
Purpose: Express SDG governance in COBIT-style outcomes, metrics, and evidence.

## 1) Governance focus for this portfolio
SDG is used as an anchor system to demonstrate governance outcomes that map to common COBIT management/governance themes:
- Risk optimization (risk identification, rating, treatment, acceptance)
- Security management (access control, cryptography, logging, monitoring)
- Change governance (release gates, review discipline, CI evidence)
- Internal control monitoring (control testing, issues/MAP tracking)

## 2) Scorecard (what leadership would care about)
| Domain | What “good” looks like | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Risk optimization | Top risks are ranked, treated, and reviewed on cadence | Risk Register + Treatment Plan + MAP |
| Security management | Access controls operate as designed and are evidenced | CT-01/02/03 + exports |
| Change governance | No release/demo without change review evidence | Change Review Checklist + CI pass |
| Control monitoring | Findings are tracked to closure with proof | Findings Register + MAP Tracker |
| Supplier governance | Vendors are assessed and tracked | TPRM pack + Vendor Register |
| Compliance governance | Obligations are known and monitored | Obligations Register + monthly review notes |

## 3) Governance KPIs (aligned to scorecard)
- Evidence Freshness % (target ≥ 90%)
- Remediation On-time % (target ≥ 90%)
- Overdue High risks (target 0)
- High findings past due (target 0)
- % releases/demos with change review evidence (target ≥ 95%)
