# Compliance Management System (CMS) Charter — SDG

Owner: Badr Karim  
Standard alignment: ISO 37301 (topic-based)  
Purpose: Establish a practical compliance operating model for SDG deployments.

## 1) Purpose
Maintain an organized system to identify, track, and evidence compliance obligations relevant to SDG deployments (security, privacy, and sector-specific rules where applicable).

## 2) Scope
In scope:
- Compliance obligation identification and tracking (obligations register)
- Mapping obligations to controls/policies and evidence
- Periodic monitoring and review cadence
- Training/awareness record (portfolio baseline)
- Escalation and remediation tracking for compliance gaps

Out of scope (portfolio baseline):
- Formal legal opinions or counsel review
- Production contractual negotiations (unless added later)

## 3) Roles and responsibilities (portfolio model)
- Compliance Owner: maintains obligations register and monitoring cadence
- Control Owners: implement and operate controls/policies supporting obligations
- Approver: approves compliance decisions, risk acceptance, and exceptions

(For this portfolio: Badr Karim performs all roles.)

## 4) Compliance lifecycle (operating model)
1) Identify obligations (laws, regulations, contracts, customer requirements)
2) Assess applicability (what deployments/industries trigger which obligations)
3) Define controls/policies required to meet obligations
4) Collect evidence and maintain it on cadence
5) Monitor changes and respond to gaps (issue log + MAP)
6) Report status monthly (GRC report + obligations review notes)

## 5) Cadence
- Monthly: obligations register review + changes assessment
- Quarterly: training/awareness record entry (portfolio baseline)
- Per release/demo: confirm key compliance-related controls have evidence

## 6) Required outputs
- Obligations Register (CSV)
- Evidence links (GRC_Master.xlsx Evidence Tracker)
- Issues & Remediation (MAP) for compliance gaps
